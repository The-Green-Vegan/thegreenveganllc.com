About

Built around a
plant-based life.

The Green Vegan LLC is the parent company behind Tendrils and Kind Gifts Co. — two distinct brands with a shared foundation.

We started from a simple idea: the choices we make every day — who we connect with, what we give, how we live — can be kinder to animals, to each other, and to the planet. Tendrils brings that idea to dating and community. Kind Gifts Co. brings it to gifting.

Based in Chicago, Illinois, we run both brands under one roof, with the same standards across everything we do: plant-based, cruelty-free, and made with care.

What Guides Us

Principles we
don’t compromise on.

These aren’t slogans on a wall. They shape what we build, what we sell, and who we build it for — across both brands.

01

Plant-Based

Plant-based living is our foundation — in the community we foster and the products we stand behind.

02

Cruelty-Free

No animal harm, no exceptions. Compassion for animals guides our choices from the start.

03

Sustainability

We favor thoughtful, lower-impact choices — made to last, made responsibly, and made with the future in mind.

Kindness

Kindness to people, animals, and the planet — in how we treat customers, community, and each other.
